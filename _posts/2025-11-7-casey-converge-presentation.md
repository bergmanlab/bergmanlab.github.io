Bergman Lab PI Casey Bergman presented on a new inititive to develop a Computational Biology Consulting Group in the UGA [Institute of Bioinformatics](https://iob.uga.edu/) at the [2025 Precision One Health Initiative Symposium](https://vet.uga.edu/event/uga-precision-one-health-symposium-25/). Casey's platform talk was presented during the Systems Modeling and Data Analytics Core session and was entitled "Leveraging Local Bioinformatics Expertise to Enhance Bioscience Research Capacity at UGA."
