A collaborative effort between the [Rozario](https://www.rozariolab.org/), [Ye](http://yelab.genetics.uga.edu), and Bergman labs led by Saurav Choudhary to sequence and annotate the genome of the rat tapeworm *Hymenolepis diminuta* has been accepted as a Genome Report at [G3](https://doi.org/10.1093/g3journal/jkag298). Congrats to Saurav for doing the lion's share of the work on this challenging project!
